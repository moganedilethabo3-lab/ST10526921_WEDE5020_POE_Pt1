Why you should adopt a dog

One of Paw R Us SA's core missions is to provide a safe and loving environment for animals in need. The organisation believes that every animal deserves a place to call home, and we are committed to making that a reality for as many animals as possible.

Though some dogs have experienced neglect, abandoment and terribles past it does not mean their past should define their future. Because through patience, love an immense kindness the lives of these dogs could be changed for the better, granting them the opportunity to be loved and given a second chance in life.


Thus, when you adopt a dog you dont just give a home to stay but a second chance to experience the goodness in living. Hence, use that power to create opportunities for these dogs and adopt today. Give a deserving dog a family it has long been waiting for and a new preview of life that they have never experienced before. Become a dog's Hero and adopt one today!


Become part of the Paw R Us SA family!

There are various ways in becoming a memeber of the Paw family. But one of the most common ways many of the public have become part of us is through volunteering.


For the past years the organisation has relied heavily on the kindess and dedication of many volunteers who have given their time to assist grow and support the animals at Paw R Us SA. And it because of our volunteers that today the organisation remains to continue its job to rescue and care for animals in need of our services.

When volunteering at Paw R Us SA you should expect to complete the following tasks as a volunteer at Paw R Us SA:


  • Assisting with animal care
  • Help raise funds for the organisation
  • Promote animal-welare awareness
