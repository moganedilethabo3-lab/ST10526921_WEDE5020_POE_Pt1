<h3>Who is Paw R Us SA? </h3>
 Paw R Us SA is a pet welfare organization that works to promote animal welfare in the community. This organization can be considered to be an NPO because their services are all assigned to assist animals in need whereby, they don't gain nor granted any benefits for their work but rather they do it for the fulfilment and goodness of their hearts. 
 
<h3> My services for Paw R Us SA </h3>
What I had noticed when doing research for an NPO was that Paw R Us SA had no official hosted website on the internet but rather they engaged with the public online using social media platforms such as Instagram and Facebook. 
Hence, I had decided to offer my services to them, to build them a website using the information I am able to find on the internet regarding the existence and activities.
